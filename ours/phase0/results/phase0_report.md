# Phase 0: EarSet (30 people, in-ear IMU, left ear)

Generated by `phase0/analyse.py` from `results/bouts.csv` (`phase0/build_bouts.py`). A bout = one burst of motion above the person's own quiet level (from their `still` recording), cut automatically (`phase0/segment.py`); continuous motion is split into pieces of at most 12 s. The first 3 nod bouts per person are used only to find the head frame and are left out of everything below. Bouts are cut offline from whole recordings, so this tests the classifier, not a live detector.

## A. How visible is each motion

Head motions were cued about every 5 s (about 24 bursts per 2-minute file, judged from the plots; EarSet does not state the count). For face motions, few bouts per person means most of them did not rise above the person's still level. For negatives (talking, walking, ...) bouts are simply what a detector would fire on.

## B. What a feature measures: the gesture or the person?

Share of each feature's variance (nod, shake, tilt bouts) explained by the gesture, by the person (person-to-person differences within the same gesture) and by repetition-to-repetition noise. A good feature has a large `gesture` share and a small `person` share.
